Pick a ring and a wheel, put the pen in a hole, and let it roll.

Tap the paper to pause. Drag clockwise around it to turn the wheel by hand.

The steps are numbered to make a first drawing easy. You don't have to follow them in order: come back to any step at any time.

Quick start

One tap draws a finished design on blank paper. Its settings stay, so you can change them and draw more on top.
